# Candidates — One Emoji, One Smile

The entry is exactly one emoji: the one most likely to make a stranger smile.
Each candidate is duelled against the judge's criteria, in order:

1. A single emoji a stranger reads as warm and friendly, most likely to make someone smile.
2. Warmth at first glance: kind, friendly or gently funny, no context needed.
3. Universality: any age/background reads it the same way; no irony or in-joke that could land wrong.
4. Chosen rather than default: feels picked for the moment, not the first smiley that comes to mind, while instantly readable.
5. Right for a stranger: no intimacy, sarcasm or flirtation that reads oddly from a stranger.

All finalists are a single Unicode code point, so the 1–8 character gate is met with room to spare.

## The field

- **😊 (smiling face with smiling eyes)** — Warm and universal (1–3 pass easily), but it is the
  textbook default smiley: criterion 4 is written about exactly this emoji. Eliminated on 4.
- **😄 (grinning face with smiling eyes)** — Bigger joy than 😊, but still a stock face and
  close to a default pick; slightly manic energy for a stranger. Eliminated on 4, weak on 2.
- **🤗 (hugging face)** — Very warm, hands open in welcome. But a hug offered by a stranger
  brushes against criterion 5: for some readers it tips from friendly into familiar. Strong
  contender; loses the final on 5.
- **🥹 (face holding back tears)** — Reads as moved/grateful, but half its readings are sad or
  overwhelmed; not a reliable smile trigger and needs context. Eliminated on 2 and 3.
- **🫶 (heart hands)** — Sweet, but it is a heart gesture: intimacy-coded and trend-coded,
  so it can land as flirtation or fandom from a stranger. Eliminated on 5 and 3.
- **☀️ (sun)** — Warm and universal, but as U+2600 + variation selector it spends two code
  points to say less than 🌞 says with one: a bare symbol, no face smiling back. Loses to 🌞 on 1.
- **🐶 (dog face)** — Dogs make most people smile instantly. Held back on 3: in some cultures
  dogs read as unclean or frightening, so it is not quite the same emoji everywhere. Semifinalist.
- **🐱 (cat face)** — Cute, but cat people vs. dog people is a real split, and the flat cat
  face can read aloof. Eliminated on 3.
- **🐣 (hatching chick)** — Genuinely smile-inducing and gentle. Slight Easter/spring
  seasonality and a hint of fragility keep it a notch below the finalists. Semifinalist.
- **🦊 (fox face)** — Charming, but foxes carry sly/trickster connotations in many traditions;
  a stranger can read mischief instead of warmth. Eliminated on 3.
- **🌈 (rainbow)** — Hopeful and bright, universally positive; but it reads as a symbol or a
  statement more than a friendly face, and carries association baggage in some contexts.
  Loses on 1 and 2 to the finalists.
- **🍀 (four-leaf clover)** — A kind wish of luck, warm in intent; but the wish needs a beat
  of interpretation, and some read it as plain clover/plant. Eliminated on 2.
- **🎈 (balloon)** — Playful and light; reads more as "party/celebration" than as warmth
  directed at the viewer. Eliminated on 1.
- **🌻 (sunflower)** — A flower that turns to face the sun: cheerful, warm, zero intimacy,
  zero sarcasm, read the same way everywhere. No face, so the smile it gives is the viewer's
  own. Finalist.
- **🌞 (sun with face)** — A smiling sun. It has the one thing 🌻 lacks — a face smiling back
  at you — with none of the default-smiley baggage of 😊. Finalist.

## Final duel: 🌞 vs 🌻

- Criterion 1: 🌞 wins. A face mid-smile is the most direct smile trigger there is; 🌻 is
  lovely scenery, 🌞 is company.
- Criterion 2: even — both are warmth at first glance, no context needed.
- Criterion 3: even — the sun means a good day in every culture and every age group; a
  sunflower likewise.
- Criterion 4: even — neither is anyone's reflexive first smiley; both feel picked.
- Criterion 5: even — neither carries intimacy, sarcasm or flirtation.

🌞 takes it on the first and most important criterion: it smiles *at* the stranger.

## WINNER

**🌞** — sun with face (U+1F31E, 1 code point)

## RUNNER-UP

**🌻** — sunflower (U+1F33B, 1 code point)
